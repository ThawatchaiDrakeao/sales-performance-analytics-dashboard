# 🧭 Dashboard User Guide

This guide is designed to help business stakeholders and management navigate the **Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard**. The dashboard is consolidated into a single, high-impact **Executive Overview** page for quick decision-making.

## 📌 Page: Executive Overview

This central hub provides a 360-degree view of the company's sales health. It is divided into several analytical sections:

### 1. KPI Summary Cards (Top Left)

- **What it shows:** The absolute total `Sum of Sales` (e.g., 1.49M) and `Sum of Profit` (e.g., 305.48K) for the selected period.
- **How to use it:** Use this as a quick pulse-check on overall business performance. These numbers will update dynamically when you interact with other charts.

### 2. Product Category Analysis (Middle Left - Pie Chart)

- **What it shows:** The breakdown of total sales distributed across different product categories (e.g., Sportwear, Bath Clothes, Children's wear).
- **How to use it:** Identify which product lines are driving the most revenue. Click on any slice (e.g., Sportwear) to filter the entire dashboard and see which employees sold those specific products and in which countries.

### 3. Temporal Trends (Bottom Left - Bar Chart)

- **What it shows:** The total `Sum of Sales` aggregated by `Year` (2019 - 2022).
- **How to use it:** Track year-over-year growth. A sharp decline in the latest year may indicate incomplete YTD data or a critical business issue requiring immediate attention.

### 4. Employee Leaderboard (Top Right - Bar Chart)

- **What it shows:** A ranked list of sales representatives based on their total generated sales.
- **How to use it:** Instantly recognize top-performing staff (e.g., Mrs. Margaret Peacock). Click on an employee's bar to see their specific geographic performance on the map and their strongest product categories.

### 5. Geographic Distribution (Bottom Right - Map)

- **What it shows:** Sales performance visualized across different countries and regions globally.
- **How to use it:** Hover over the data points (bubbles) on the map to see specific regional sales figures. Useful for planning territory expansions or targeted regional marketing campaigns.

## 🔄 Interactivity & Filtering

All visuals on this dashboard are strictly interconnected.

- **Cross-Filtering:** Clicking a bar in the Employee Leaderboard will automatically filter the Pie Chart and the Map to show data _only_ related to that specific employee.
- **To Reset:** Click on the same data point again, or click on any empty white space within the dashboard canvas to clear all active filters.
