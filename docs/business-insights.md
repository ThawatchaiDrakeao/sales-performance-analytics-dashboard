## 📝 Executive Summary

Based on the Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard, the company has generated a total of **1.49M in Sales** and **305.48K in Profit** over the analyzed period. However, there are fluctuations in yearly sales that require strategic attention.

- **Sportwear Dominance:** `Sportwear` is the absolute market leader, contributing a massive **38.06% (567.99K)** of total sales.

- **Underperformers:** Categories like `Babywear` and `Men's Clothes` contribute significantly less to the overall revenue stream.
